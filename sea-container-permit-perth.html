Question answer

Do you need a permit for a sea container in Perth?

Short answer

Usually yes, unless it's short-term. Many Perth councils have their own sea container policy, and it's often stricter than the shed rules. Cockburn doesn't allow containers on home lots except during building works. Wanneroo needs planning approval unless the container meets its standard rules. A container used as a building may need a building permit too.

Last updated 6 November 2026

Why containers are different

Perth councils often have a separate planning policy just for sea containers (also called shipping containers). They don't treat a container like a normal garden shed, so the shed size rules you may have read about don't simply apply. The City of Wanneroo, for example, says containers are not assessed as "outbuildings" under the R-Codes.

Conditions councils usually set

  • Behind the house and screened from the street and neighbours
  • Painted to match the house or the landscape, and kept in good repair
  • Used for storage only (Cockburn rules out living, business or industrial use)
  • Not in car parking bays or blocking access
  • No refrigerated containers on home or rural lots (Cockburn)

Does it need a building permit too?

Often, yes, if it's set up and used as a building. Building and Energy says there are no special exemptions for prefabricated buildings, and councils can take action if one goes up without the right approvals. A container fitted out as a workshop, studio or room is the clearest case. Any power or plumbing must be done by licensed trades.

If you want to live or work in it, the rules get much stricter. Wanneroo says a container used for habitable purposes must meet all relevant laws, like any other home.

What to do

  1. Check your council's sea container policy (search "[your council] sea container policy").
  2. If it needs planning approval, apply for that first.
  3. Then check whether a building permit is needed for how you'll use it.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need council approval for a sea container in Perth?

Usually, yes, unless it's short-term. Many councils have a sea container policy. Cockburn doesn't allow them on residential lots except during building works, and Wanneroo needs planning approval unless the container meets its standard rules.

Can I keep a shipping container in my backyard?

It depends on your council. Some allow it with planning approval and conditions like screening and painting. Others, like Cockburn, don't allow them on residential lots except while you build.

How long can a sea container stay on my property?

In Wanneroo, up to 7 days for moving house, or during building works until 14 days after they finish. In Cockburn, up to 18 months during building works, with planning approval.

Does a shipping container need a building permit?

Often, if it's used as a building. Building and Energy says there are no special exemptions for prefabricated buildings. Check with your council.

Can I live in a shipping container in WA?

Only if it meets all the rules for a home. Wanneroo says containers used for habitable purposes must comply with all relevant laws, and Cockburn's policy limits them to storage.
