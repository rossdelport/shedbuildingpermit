City of Swan shed rules

Short answer

Sheds over 10 m² or 2.4 m high need a building permit. On a house block, planning approval usually isn't needed up to 60 m² (or 10% of the lot) with 2.4 m walls and 1 m from the boundary. Rural lots get bigger exemptions: up to 300 m² in General Rural and Swan Valley Rural, and 200 m² in Rural Living and similar zones.

Last updated 8 November 2026

Building permit

The City of Swan follows the state building rules. A freestanding shed of 10 m² or less and no more than 2.4 m high doesn't need a building permit. Anything bigger usually does. You can lodge online through the City's ePathway portal, and the City has 25 business days to decide an uncertified (BA2) application, or 10 business days for a certified (BA1) one.

Planning approval on a house block

On residential lots, sheds usually don't need planning approval if they meet the R-Codes' standard ("deemed-to-comply") rules for outbuildings. These are the same across WA:

  • Total outbuildings no more than 60 m² or 10% of the lot, whichever is less
  • Walls no higher than 2.4 m and the ridge no higher than 4.2 m
  • Usually at least 1 m from side and rear boundaries
  • Not in the street setback, and not used to live in

The City's own development application notices describe a 1 m setback and 2.4 m wall height as "permitted as of right". Bigger or taller sheds need planning approval. Heritage areas, heritage-listed places and lots near the Swan River usually need it too.

Rural and Swan Valley lots

The City of Swan's planning scheme (Local Planning Scheme No. 17, Schedule 5) exempts bigger rural sheds from planning approval if they meet all its conditions:

Even when planning approval isn't needed, a shed this size still needs a building permit.

Before you apply

  • Check your zoning and R-Code on the City's maps.
  • Check for sewer pipes and easements. See building a shed near a sewer.
  • The City also has a "Shed, Patio, Sea-Container and Water Tank Questionnaire" to help work out what approvals you need.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a shed in the City of Swan?

A freestanding shed of 10 m² or less and up to 2.4 m high doesn't need a building permit. Bigger sheds usually do. Planning approval is only needed if the shed doesn't meet the R-Codes or scheme rules.

How big can a shed be in the City of Swan without planning approval?

On a house block, usually up to 60 m² of total outbuildings (or 10% of the lot), with 2.4 m walls and a 4.2 m ridge. On General Rural and Swan Valley Rural land, up to 300 m²; on Special Rural, Rural Living and similar zones, up to 200 m².

How long does a City of Swan shed permit take?

The City has 25 business days to decide an uncertified (BA2) application and 10 business days for a certified (BA1) one.

How close to the fence can a shed be in the City of Swan?

Usually 1 m from side and rear boundaries to meet the standard R-Codes rules on a house block. Closer needs planning approval. Rural zones have their own setbacks.

Do rural sheds in the City of Swan need a building permit?

Yes. The planning exemption for rural sheds doesn't remove the need for a building permit.
