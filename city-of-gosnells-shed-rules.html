City of Gosnells shed rules

Short answer

In the City of Gosnells, a shed needs a building permit unless it's freestanding, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or lower. All sheds together are capped at 60 m² or 10% of the lot, whichever is less, with 2.4 m walls and a 4.2 m roof. Keep at least 1 m from side and rear boundaries.

Last updated 27 November 2026

Do you need a building permit?

Yes, unless the shed is freestanding, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or lower. A garage follows the same rule in Gosnells. Exempt sheds must still meet the building code and the R-Codes.

Size and height

LimitRule
Wall height2.4 m
Roof height4.2 m
Total area of all sheds60 m² or 10% of the lot, whichever is less

So on a 450 m² lot, the most you can have is 45 m² of sheds in total. Bigger sheds can be considered with a development application under the City's Local Planning Policy 2.2 (Outbuildings and Sea Containers). Check with the shed size checker.

Setbacks

BoundaryMinimum setback
Side and rear, walls up to 9 m long1 m
Side and rear, walls over 9 m long1.5 m
Secondary street1.5 m

A smaller setback can be considered with a development application, the fee and comments from your neighbours. Easements can't be built over. If there's a 2 m easement across the back, the shed must be at least 2 m from the rear boundary. See how close a shed can be to the fence.

What to send

  • A BA2 form and the fees.
  • A site plan at 1:200 or better with street names, lot and house numbers, lot size, the shed's size and position, and setbacks to all boundaries.
  • At least one elevation at 1:100 or better.
  • Structural details: beams, purlins, columns, connections, footings, slab thickness and roof sheeting.
  • At least 2 m head clearance from the paving to the underside of the main beam.

See shed site plans. Call Building Services on 9397 3000.

Questions

Common questions

What size shed can I build without a permit in Gosnells?

A freestanding shed of 10 m² or less and no more than 2.4 m high.

How big can a shed be in the City of Gosnells?

All sheds on a lot together can't exceed 60 m² or 10% of the lot area, whichever is less, unless approved through a development application.

How far from the fence does a shed need to be in Gosnells?

At least 1 m from side and rear boundaries for walls up to 9 m long, 1.5 m for longer walls, and 1.5 m from a secondary street.

Can I build a shed over an easement in Gosnells?

No. Setbacks set by easements can't be varied, so the shed must stay clear of the easement.
