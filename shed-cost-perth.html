Cost guide

What a Perth shed really costs, approvals included

Short answer

Perth suppliers advertise about $2,200 to $4,200 installed for a small 3 x 3 m garden shed and $16,000 to $30,000 for a 6 x 6 m double garage. State permit fees are small: a $20,000 shed lodged on a BA2 pays $182.65. Engineering, the slab and site work make up more of the cost.

Last updated 11 November 2026

The approval costs, in detail

The council's part is set by state rules and is smaller than most people expect:

ItemAmount
Uncertified permit fee (BA2)0.32% of the value, minimum $121
Certified permit fee (BA1)0.19% of the value, minimum $121, plus a private building surveyor's fee
Building Services Levy$61.65 for work up to $45,000
Structural engineeringSome kit suppliers include it; otherwise the engineer's fee
Council extrasCouncils vary. Wanneroo, for example, needs a Street and Verge Bond Permit with every building permit.

Example: a $20,000 shed lodged on a BA2 pays the $121 minimum fee plus the $61.65 levy, so $182.65 in state fees. The value has to include labour, materials and GST, not just the kit.

Costs people forget

  • Site levelling, tree clearing and access for the truck
  • Drainage, gutters and downpipes
  • Extra doors, windows and insulation
  • A bushfire (BAL) assessment if the shed is near the house in a bushfire-prone area
  • Planning approval if the shed doesn't meet the R-Codes or your council's rules

Questions

Common questions

How much does a shed cost in Perth?

Phoenix Patios advertises $2,200 to $4,200 installed for a 3 x 3 m garden shed and $16,000 to $30,000 for a 6 x 6 m double garage. Modern Steel Buildings lists 6 x 6 m kits from $10,385.

How much are council fees for a shed in WA?

An uncertified permit (BA2) is 0.32% of the value with a $121 minimum, plus a $61.65 levy for work up to $45,000. A $20,000 shed pays $182.65 in state fees.

Is a kit shed cheaper than an installed shed?

Usually. Phoenix Patios says doing it yourself can save 20 to 40% on labour. You still need the slab, engineering and permit.

Does the value for the permit include labour?

Yes. The value must include goods, labour, services and GST, not just the kit price.
