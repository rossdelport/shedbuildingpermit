Shed vs garage: what approvals each needs

Short answer

Both are Class 10a buildings and need a building permit (except sheds of 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or lower). The difference is planning: sheds follow the outbuilding rules (size, height, 1 m from boundaries), while garages follow street setback and boundary wall rules.

What both need

Both are Class 10a buildings under the building code, so the building permit process is the same: plans, engineering, a BA1 or BA2 application, and council fees of 0.32% of the value for a BA2 (at least $121). Since 1 July 2026, a Class 10a building worth less than $50,000 doesn't have to be built by a registered builder, and you can build it yourself without owner-builder approval.

Sheds: the outbuilding rules

At councils like Wanneroo and Melville, a shed skips planning approval if it's no more than 60 m² or 10% of the lot, walls are no higher than 2.4 m and the ridge no higher than 4.2 m, and it's at least 1 m from side and rear boundaries. Go bigger or taller and you'll need planning approval.

Garages: the street and boundary rules

At the City of Stirling, a garage can be as close as 4.5 m to the front boundary, or closer if it's at least 0.5 m behind the front of the house. A garage wall can go on the side boundary on R20 lots and above, but must sit behind the primary street setback (6 m on R20 and R25, 4 m on R30 and R40). Minimum size is 5.4 m long and 2.4 m wide for a single, or 4.8 m wide for a double.

Freestanding garages

A garage that isn't attached to the house doesn't fit the R-Codes garage definition. Ask your council whether it will assess yours under its garage rules or its outbuilding (shed) rules, as this changes the size and height limits.

Questions

Common questions

Do sheds and garages need the same permit?

Both are Class 10a buildings and need a building permit, apart from small sheds of 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or lower. The planning rules are different: sheds follow outbuilding rules and garages follow street setback and boundary wall rules.

Do I need a registered builder for a garage in WA?

Not if it's worth less than $50,000. From 1 July 2026, Class 10a buildings like garages, sheds, carports and patios under $50,000 don't need a registered builder.

How close to the street can a garage be?

At the City of Stirling, 4.5 m from the front boundary, or closer if the garage is at least 0.5 m behind the front of the house.

Is a freestanding garage a shed?

It doesn't fit the R-Codes garage definition, which covers garages attached to the house. Ask your council which rules it will use.
