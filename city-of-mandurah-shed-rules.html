City of Mandurah shed rules

Short answer

In the City of Mandurah, a shed needs a building permit unless it's freestanding, under 10 m² and 2.4 m or lower (one per lot). Total shed area is capped at 10% of lots under 600 m², or 60 m² plus 2.5 m² per extra 100 m² on bigger lots. Walls are generally limited to 3.0 m.

Last updated 26 November 2026

Do you need a building permit?

Yes, unless the shed is freestanding, under 10 m² and 2.4 m or lower, and it meets the setback rules. Only one small exempt shed is allowed per lot. A garage always needs a building permit.

Planning approval may not be needed, depending on your zoning, site cover, the shed's size and height, and any special conditions. Ask a City planning officer to confirm.

How big can it be?

Lot sizeTotal shed area allowed
Under 600 m²Up to 10% of the lot (500 m² lot = 50 m²)
Over 600 m²60 m², plus 2.5 m² for every 100 m² over 600 m² (800 m² lot = 65 m²)

The limit is the combined area of all sheds and outbuildings on the lot. Try the shed size checker.

Height

  • Walls: generally up to 3.0 m.
  • Walls in zincalume, unpainted fibre cement or similar: up to 2.4 m.
  • Roof: generally up to 4.2 m.

Setbacks and other rules

Setbacks come from the City's planning scheme, the R-Codes, local planning policies and any detailed area plan. Different density zones can have different setbacks, and some zones also need minimum open space. Check your zoning on the City's online map, and confirm setbacks with Building Services. See how close a shed can be to the fence.

  • Sheds generally aren't allowed on vacant residential lots.
  • Bigger sheds or reduced setbacks usually need a development application and neighbour consultation, approved before the building permit.

What to send

  • A BA1 (with a Certificate of Design Compliance) or BA2 form.
  • A site plan at 1:200 or better, with setbacks to boundaries and all buildings, pools and retaining walls, and ground levels.
  • Floor plan, section and elevations at 1:100 or better.
  • Construction details and how the shed is anchored, such as footings. See shed site plans.

Lodge online via "Building Lodgements" on the City's website. Building Services is on 9550 3777.

Questions

Common questions

What size shed can I build without a permit in Mandurah?

A freestanding shed under 10 m² and no more than 2.4 m high, that meets the setbacks. Only one is allowed per lot.

How big can a shed be in Mandurah?

On lots under 600 m², up to 10% of the lot. On bigger lots, 60 m² plus 2.5 m² for every 100 m² over 600 m², counting all sheds together.

How high can a shed be in Mandurah?

Walls generally up to 3.0 m, or 2.4 m if finished in zincalume or unpainted fibre cement. The roof is generally limited to 4.2 m.

Can I put a shed on a vacant block in Mandurah?

Generally not. The City says sheds are generally not permitted on vacant residential lots.
