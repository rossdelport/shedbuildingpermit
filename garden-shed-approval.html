Question

Do I need council approval for a garden shed?

Short answer

Usually no building permit if the shed is freestanding, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or less high. Councils such as Joondalup, Melville, Gosnells and Bayswater publish this. You still need to follow planning rules, like keeping it out of the front setback.

Last updated 12 November 2026

The small shed exemption

Many Perth councils say a garden shed doesn't need a building permit if it is:

  • Freestanding (not attached to the house or another building)
  • 10 m² or less in floor area
  • 2.4 m or less in height

The City of Joondalup adds that it must be installed to the manufacturer's instructions. The City of Melville adds that it must not be in wind region C or D. Gosnells, Kwinana, Bayswater and Mundaring publish the same 10 m² and 2.4 m limits. Councils vary, so check yours.

A 3 x 3 m shed is 9 m², so it usually fits. A 3 x 3.5 m shed is 10.5 m², so it usually doesn't.

Planning rules still apply

No building permit doesn't mean anything goes. The City of Joondalup says a small shed of 10 m² or less only skips planning approval if:

  • No more than two walls are within 600 mm of a boundary
  • It's not in the street setback (in front of the house)
  • Walls and ridge are no higher than 2.7 m
  • You keep enough open space and outdoor living area

Bigger sheds in Joondalup

For larger sheds, Joondalup's deemed-to-comply limits are: no more than 60 m² or 10% of the site (whichever is less), walls up to 2.4 m, ridge up to 4.2 m, and set back 1 m from side and rear boundaries (1.5 m if the wall is longer than 14 m).

Questions

Common questions

Do I need council approval for a garden shed in Perth?

Usually not a building permit if it's freestanding, 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or less high. Planning rules like setbacks still apply, and councils vary.

Is a 3 x 3 m shed exempt?

Usually yes. It's 9 m², under the 10 m² limit, as long as it's freestanding and no higher than 2.4 m.

Can I put a garden shed against the fence?

Joondalup allows a small shed with up to two walls within 600 mm of a boundary without planning approval. Councils vary.

Can I put a garden shed in my front yard?

Usually not without planning approval. Joondalup says a small shed in the street setback needs it.
