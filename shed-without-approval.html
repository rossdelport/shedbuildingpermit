Question answer

What happens if you build a shed without approval?

Short answer

Owners must get a building permit before building, and penalties apply if they don't. You can fix it with a building approval certificate (BA13), backed by a building surveyor's inspection (BA18). It costs 0.38% of the value (at least $121) plus a 0.274% levy and the surveyor's fee. Or take it down.

Last updated 5 November 2026

Why it matters

Building and Energy says owners must make sure a building permit is obtained before work starts, and penalties apply if it isn't. Most sheds need one: anything bigger than 10 m² or taller than 2.4 m. A shed that also breaks the planning rules (too big, too tall or too close to a boundary) can need planning approval as well.

Your options if it's already built

OptionWhat it involves
Get it approved after the factApply for a building approval certificate (form BA13) with a certificate of building compliance (BA18) from a registered building surveyor, who inspects the shed
Take it downA shed of 40 m² or less, with no asbestos and not on a heritage property, can be removed without a demolition permit
Make it smallerA freestanding shed cut down to 10 m² or less and 2.4 m or lower no longer needs a building permit

What retrospective approval costs

ChargeAmount
BA13 for unauthorised work0.38% of the value of the work, at least $121
Building Services Levy for unauthorised work0.274% of the value of the work
Building surveyorTheir own fee for inspecting and certifying

For a $20,000 shed that's $121 plus $54.80 levy, plus the surveyor. The value used is the current value of the work, as decided by council. If the shed also needs planning approval, that's a separate application.

What if it doesn't meet the rules?

The surveyor certifies that the shed meets the building standards. If it doesn't, it may need changes before it can be approved. Council can also refuse planning approval for a shed that's too big or too close to a boundary.

Questions

Common questions

What happens if I build a shed without a permit in WA?

Owners must make sure a permit is obtained before building, and penalties apply if it isn't. You can fix it by getting a building approval certificate (BA13) with a building surveyor's certificate of building compliance (BA18), or by removing the shed.

Can I get a shed approved after it's built?

Yes, with a building approval certificate (BA13). A registered building surveyor inspects the shed and issues a certificate of building compliance (BA18).

How much does retrospective shed approval cost?

The BA13 fee is 0.38% of the value (at least $121) plus a 0.274% levy, plus the building surveyor's fee.

Do I need a permit to remove an unapproved shed?

Not if it's 40 m² or less, has no asbestos and the property isn't heritage-listed.
