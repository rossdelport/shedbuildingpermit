City of Joondalup shed rules

Short answer

In Joondalup, a freestanding shed up to 10 m² and 2.4 m high needs no building permit. Bigger sheds need one. Without planning approval, all sheds together can be up to 60 m² or 10% of the lot, with walls up to 2.4 m and a ridge up to 4.2 m, set back 1 m from the boundary.

Last updated 19 November 2026

Building permit

In the City of Joondalup, a shed doesn't need a building permit if it's freestanding, no bigger than 10 m², no taller than 2.4 m, and put together the way the maker says. Anything bigger needs a building permit. You can lodge online. The City has 10 business days for a certified BA1 and 25 business days for an uncertified BA2.

Small sheds (up to 10 m²)

You usually don't need planning approval for one small shed if:

  • No more than two walls are within 600 mm of a boundary.
  • Walls and roof are no higher than 2.7 m.
  • It isn't in the front or side street setback.
  • It doesn't eat into the open space and outdoor living area your lot needs.

Bigger sheds

If your shed breaks one of these, you can still apply for planning approval, which comes before the building permit. A planning application for work under $50,000 costs $147 in 2025/26. Applying after you've built costs extra, as a penalty of twice the normal fee. Councils vary, so check your lot's zoning, especially on rural or special residential land.
