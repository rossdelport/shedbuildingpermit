Planning approval vs building permit for sheds

Short answer

A building permit checks the shed is built safely, and most sheds over 10 m² or 2.4 m high need one. Planning approval checks where it goes and how big it is, and is usually only needed if the shed is over 60 m² (or 10% of the lot), has walls over 2.4 m or a ridge over 4.2 m, or sits in the front setback.

When a shed needs a building permit

Most do. Perth councils usually exempt a shed that is freestanding, no bigger than 10 m² and no taller than 2.4 m. Anything bigger needs a building permit. A small exempt shed must still be built properly.

When a shed also needs planning approval

Under the standard R-Codes rules for outbuildings, a backyard shed on a house lot usually doesn't need planning approval if it:

  • is no bigger than 60 m² or 10% of the lot (whichever is less), counting all your outbuildings together,
  • has walls no higher than 2.4 m and a ridge no higher than 4.2 m,
  • isn't in the front setback, and
  • meets the side and rear setbacks and leaves enough open space.

Go over any of those and you'll usually need planning approval first, then the building permit. Councils vary. For example, the City of Bayswater's Residential Development Policy (September 2026) allows outbuilding walls up to 3.0 m. Heritage areas, rural zones and special estates can have their own rules too.

Which comes first?

If you need both, get planning approval first. The building permit has to match what was approved, and councils won't usually issue it until planning conditions are met.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need planning approval for a shed in WA?

Usually not, if it's no bigger than 60 m² (or 10% of the lot), walls no higher than 2.4 m, ridge no higher than 4.2 m, not in the front setback and meets setbacks. Councils vary.

Do I need a building permit for a shed in WA?

Yes, unless it's freestanding, no bigger than 10 m² and no taller than 2.4 m.

What's the difference between planning approval and a building permit?

Planning approval checks where the shed goes and how big it is. A building permit checks that it's built safely to the building code.

Which do I get first?

Planning approval, if you need it. The building permit follows and must match the approved plans.
