City of Armadale shed rules

Short answer

In the City of Armadale, a shed needs a building permit unless it's under 10 m², freestanding and no higher than 2.4 m. Sheds can't go in the front setback area. The City decides certified applications in up to 10 business days and uncertified ones in up to 25.

Last updated 28 November 2026

Do you need a building permit?

Usually yes. The City of Armadale says a shed or garage doesn't need a permit only if it is all of these:

  • less than 10 m² in floor area,
  • freestanding, and
  • no higher than 2.4 m.

Even a permit-free shed must meet the building code, including fire separation from boundaries and other buildings (for example, at least 900 mm). Check yours with the shed size checker.

Where it can go

Sheds aren't allowed in the front setback area, which is the front yard forward of the house line. Other setbacks follow the City's local planning policies and the R-Codes. Planning approval may be needed before a building permit can be issued. See how close a shed can be to the fence.

Who can build it

The City's June 2026 sheet says a registered builder or owner-builder approval is needed if the build cost is $50,000 or more.

How long it takes

ApplicationWho checks itCity's time limit
Certified (BA1)A private building surveyor signs a certificate firstUp to 10 business days
Uncertified (BA2)The City's building surveyorUp to 25 business days

The permit lasts 2 years. When the shed is finished, the builder lodges a BA7 notice of completion within 7 days.

What to send

  • A BA1 or BA2 form, signed by the builder and the applicant, and the fees.
  • A site plan at 1:200 showing the shed, existing buildings, boundary setbacks, fire separation and any septic tanks or leach drains.
  • A floor plan, elevations and cross-section at 1:100.
  • A structural engineer's certificate.
  • A bushfire (BAL) assessment and planning approval, if needed.
  • A Construction Training Fund levy receipt if the work is over $100,000.

See shed site plans. Call the City on 9394 5000.

Questions

Common questions

What size shed can I build without a permit in Armadale?

One that is less than 10 m², freestanding and no higher than 2.4 m. It must still meet the building code, including fire separation.

Can I put a shed in my front yard in Armadale?

No. The City says outbuildings aren't permitted in the front setback area, forward of the house line.

How long does a shed permit take in Armadale?

Up to 10 business days for a certified (BA1) application, or up to 25 business days for an uncertified (BA2) one, unless more information is needed.

Do I need a registered builder for a shed in Armadale?

Only if the build cost is $50,000 or more. Below that, you can build it yourself with a permit.
