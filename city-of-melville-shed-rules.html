City of Melville shed rules

Short answer

In Melville, a freestanding shed needs a building permit if it's over 10 m², over 2.4 m high, or in wind region C or D. Planning approval usually isn't needed if it's no more than 60 m² (or 10% of the site), walls 2.4 m, ridge 4.2 m, and 1 m from side and rear boundaries.

Last updated 13 November 2026

When you need a building permit

In Melville, a freestanding shed needs a building permit if any of these apply:

  • The floor area is more than 10 m²
  • It is more than 2.4 m high
  • It's in wind region C or D

The City says a freestanding shed can go right up to the boundary, but must be at least 900 mm from a house wall or 450 mm from the house eaves to meet the building code's fire rules. If that can't be done, the shed must be 900 mm from the boundary.

When you need planning approval

In a residential zone, planning approval is usually not needed if the shed:

  • Isn't attached to the house, and isn't lived in
  • Together with other sheds is no more than 60 m² or 10% of the site, whichever is less
  • Has walls no higher than 2.4 m and a ridge no higher than 4.2 m
  • Isn't in the front or side street setback (6 m at R15 to R25; usually 3 m to a side street)
  • Is 1 m from side and rear boundaries (closer may be allowed, so ask the City)
  • Keeps enough open space (50% at R15 to R25, 45% at R30 to R40, 55% at R12.5)
  • Isn't on a heritage-listed property

In other zones, planning approval is always needed.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need a permit for a shed in the City of Melville?

A freestanding shed needs a building permit if it's over 10 m², over 2.4 m high, or in wind region C or D.

Can I build a shed on the boundary in Melville?

The City says a freestanding shed can go up to the boundary if it's at least 900 mm from a house wall or 450 mm from the eaves. Planning rules may still need 1 m, so check with the City.

How much is a shed permit in Melville?

$166.65 for a shed under $20,000. Over that, 0.19% plus a 0.137% levy, and a $1,900 verge bond.

How big can a shed be in Melville without planning approval?

Up to 60 m² or 10% of the site (whichever is less), with walls up to 2.4 m and ridge up to 4.2 m.
